Why aren't the root nodes limited to a single hand?

Poker has 169 hand types, but the same hand can have different actions based on position, effective stack size, opponent range, and price. Post-flop, additional factors like number of players, suit, board texture, and action history must also be considered. This groups common issues and shows specific hand examples with branching; it does not directly connect 'top pair' to 'always call all-in'.
