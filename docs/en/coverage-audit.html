Coverage and open questions

The 57 lessons cover the main foundations, with 25 conditional range charts, 60 additional practice drills and the decision-tree extension. Counts and arithmetic checks do not prove complete strategic coverage or optimal expected value.

What the newer trees add

20 cash-game trees with 191 nodes now include JJ defending a 3bet out of position; KK and AKo in 4bet pots; KJs in BB versus SB; QJs in BB versus BTN; and 99, AJs and 76s in multiway or deeper situations. The newer ten trees track actions, stack changes, calls and pots. The 35 situation entries make existing decision nodes easier to find.

Remaining gaps

  • Full positional preflop response matrices, particularly BB versus SB and more out-of-position 3bet defense.
  • More multi-limper, cold-call and deep multiway continuations. Specific examples do not cover every such pot.
  • Hand-versus-range equity with future runouts, card removal, ties, multiple players and sampling error. Most existing numerical examples state their equity assumptions.
  • Complete action-EV comparisons for semi-bluffs and competing bet sizes, including both folds and equity when called.
  • More backdoor-draw turns, multi-street bluff selection, and defense against two or three barrels.
  • More strong-hand checks, range protection and counter-adjustments; additional monotone and connected 3bet/4bet boards.
  • Broader tournament early/middle-position and blind responses across stack depths, facing small 3bets or reshoves, with ante and ICM assumptions.
  • Real bounty-value conversion and larger tournament models. The ICM calculator handles at most nine players and cannot replace full-field bubble data with one table.

How to read the baselines

The BTN 20/30/40 BB opening charts deliberately share a core; the different responses to 3bets, reshoves and later streets are not fully modeled. The 200 BB AKo chart uses a call baseline against the stated reasonable non-nut SB 3bet range, without claiming that calling is solver-optimal. A tight-4bet chart without a fixed size is only a continuation screen.

Position, rake, straddles and opponent tendencies are scenario inputs. Real-table information has to be observed; missing it is not a license to apply a fixed answer.

Validation limits

Structural checks cover amounts, cards, answer indices, paths and playback state. Newer trees have additional action/stack/pot checks and selected current-nut claims were enumerated. These checks are not solver validation. Actual page layout, sound quality and playback on each browser/device require practical testing.
