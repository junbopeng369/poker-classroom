Comparing 100 BB and 200 BB Play

The same preflop bet and call will have more than double the risk after the flop.

Takeaway: First compare actual remaining stack and nut potential, then decide on edge hands versus a pair.

  • Offline 1/3 · 8–9 player table
  • Example hand explicitly notes effective chips
  • Range example is not a general GTO range

1. Same preflop bet, different future potential

BTN opens 10, SB raises to 40, BTN calls, BB folds; the pot is 83.

Starting with 300 leaves each player with 260; starting with 600 leaves each with 560. The main difference plays out on later streets, not by mechanically doubling the initial open size.

BTN opens 10, SB raises to 40, BTN calls, BB folds; the pot is 83.

Full spoken explanation

With a starting stack of three hundred chips, each player has two hundred and sixty chips left. With a starting stack of six hundred chips, each player has five hundred and sixty chips left. The main difference appears on later streets. It does not mean mechanically doubling the initial open size.
