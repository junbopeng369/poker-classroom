Postflop Planning with Medium and Short Stacks

Under low SPR, knowing what the next bet means is crucial.

Takeaway: Consider bet size, relative hand strength, and opponent range together; don't let low SPR automatically dictate action.

  • Live tournament
  • Chips and ante as per problem statement
  • Strategy must align with reward structure

1. Pot 13,000, remaining 26,000

Current SPR = 2, which could lead to a full commitment on several bets.

With shallow stacks, large bets quickly alter future pot sizes. First assess the possible bet sizes available to both sides, then determine whether your hand is suited to continue building a large pot.

2. Compare your hand against your opponent's range

You hold T♠T♦, flop J♣9♥4♠.

This is a medium-range showdown value pair. It may lead to some small pairs, draws, or air, and trail Jx and stronger hands. A low SPR by itself does not turn it into a hand that must be shove all-in.

3. Small and big bets leave different spaces

Betting 26,000 to 4,000 or 13,000 results in dramatically different subsequent stack sizes.

Bet sizing requires a purpose: which weak hands will pay, which better hands will fold, or whether you can force opponents to fold their equity. Also consider your range and pot size if facing a shove.
