Facing an Open: Call, 3bet or Fold

Include opponent position, bet size, and players behind in pre-flop decision-making.

Takeaway: Yellow is conditional continuation, not random calling; use conservative default when conditions are missing.

  • Offline 1/3 · 8–9 player table
  • Example hand explicitly notes effective chips
  • Range example is not a general GTO range

Full spoken explanation

Assume that you and the opponent each have one hundred big blinds. An early position player raises to fifteen, and the other players before you fold. You are on the button with pocket tens. Both blinds are relatively passive. Under this teaching baseline, would you flat call, shove, or fold?

4. CO wide open, the button's continuation range differs

Medium pocket pairs and some suited broadways can call. Stronger hands reraise, while marginal hands need additional conditions to continue.

This chart provides one workable simplified strategy. If the blinds squeeze often, reduce marginal flat-calls. If the opener frequently continues against 3bets with weaker hands, some suited broadways can shift toward value 3bets.

7. Yellow must correspond to a condition

A yellow AJo and KQo does not mean you should always call.

For example, if the player in position opens wide and frequently folds pairs, it may support an aggressive approach; however, if the player behind frequently squeezes, a passive call becomes harder. When no specific reason can be given, use the default fold rule from this conservative table.
