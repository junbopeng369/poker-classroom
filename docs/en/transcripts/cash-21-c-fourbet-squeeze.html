4bets and Squeeze Raises

When facing more dead money and a stronger continuation range, first calculate the investment structure.

Takeaway: Dead money is an opportunity, not a guarantee; large additional bets are only responsible for the remaining range.

  • Offline 1/3 · 8–9 player table
  • Example hand explicitly notes effective chips
  • Range example is not a general GTO range

4. Which hands primarily extract value?

AK has a value case if the opponent continues with AQ and weaker suited broadways.

If the opponent only continues with extremely strong hands, the squeeze gain relies more on folding. Marginal non-flush hands perform poorly out of position in multi-way pots and do not automatically attack just because there is dead money in the pot.

5. First screen for continuation hands against 4bet

In offline play, when opponents are tight and lack light 4bet evidence, use a dedicated tight range table.

King pairs and sharp pair hands can serve as continuation core. Queen pairs and sharp king pairs require price and opponent's re-raise range considerations; one cannot necessarily go all-in just because they raised first.
