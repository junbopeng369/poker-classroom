3betting: Purpose, Sizing and Responses

Ask who will continue before deciding how much to bet.

Takeaway: An opponent's wide open range does not imply a wide continuation range; the 3bet size must be adjusted based on position and effective stack.

  • Offline 1/3 · 8–9 player table
  • Example hand explicitly notes effective chips
  • Range example is not a general GTO range

1. After a raise, you're facing a new range.

Value 3bet hopes to extract money from weaker hands; a bluff-heavy 3bet relies on opponents folding.

A single hand can have multiple sources of equity. Evaluate whether AJo is suitable for a re-raise by considering the opponent's open, call, and re-raise ranges.

2. In-position sizing example

CO raised to 12, BTN re-raised to 36: approximately 3 times the original open.

This starting example assumes an effective 100 BB, one opener and no callers. If CO calls to a total of 36 and the blinds fold, the pot is 36+36+1+3=76, with 264 left for each player.

3. Out-of-position typically requires a different sizing approach

CO raised to 12, SB raised to 48 can serve as an example of a larger raise.

Out-of-position, opponents can more easily exploit information and price with calls. If CO calls to 48, big blind folds, pot becomes 48+48+3=99. The exact size depends on number of players, depth, and opponents and is not fixed at 4 times.

5. Before acting, consider how you'd respond to a re-raise

Already committed chips; not a reason to continue
