Positions and the Order of Play

From preflop to river, see who acts first and who gains more information.

Takeaway: Record position, action order, community cards, and effective stack before making decisions.

  • Offline 1/3 · 8–9 player table
  • Example hand explicitly notes effective chips
  • Range example is not a general GTO range

1. A hand through all streets

Preflop → Flop 3 cards → Turn 1 cards → River 1 cards.

Each street has a betting round. At showdown, choose the best five-card hand from your two hole cards and the five community cards. You can also win without a showdown when every opponent folds.

2. Button defines relative position

BTN acts after; CO is after the button, HJ is after CO.

Typically at a multi-player table, the player to the left of the small blind acts first pre-flop. When there are many players behind, the player behind gains more action information. Position is a relative relationship, not a fixed seat number.

3. After the flop, start from the left of the button again

When only BTN and BB remain, the flop, turn, and river are acted on by BB first.

The big blind usually acts late preflop, but acts before the button postflop. Heads-up, the small blind is also the button: it acts first preflop and last postflop. Check the local rules separately when a straddle is in play.

4. How does position become an advantage?

You are in BTN, opponent is in BB, only two players reach the flop.

Select the correct description. Position provides information advantage that continues through subsequent streets, but does not automatically turn a dominated hand into a profitable call.

You are in BTN, opponent is in BB, only two players reach the flop.

Full spoken explanation

Select the correct description. Position provides information advantage that continues through subsequent streets, but does not automatically turn a dominated hand into a profitable call.

Make your decision

  1. BB acts first, BTN gains more information
  2. BTN has position, so all hands can call
  3. BTN acts first, so can bet early
Answer and explanations

Suggested answer: BB acts first, BTN gains more information
Correct. Acting later lets you see opponents' actions after deciding your own.

Alternative: BTN has position, so all hands can call
Position is an advantage, but must be combined with opponent range, bet size, and number of players.

Alternative: BTN acts first, so can bet early
After the flop, small blind acts first. The button's advantage comes from acting later.
