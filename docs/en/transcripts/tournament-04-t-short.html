Decisions at 10–20 BB

Distinguish between proactive all-in, reacting to all-in, and the different logic behind small raise.

Takeaway: Short stack has multiple strategies; position, ante, opponents, and reward conditions jointly determine the choice.

2. Difference in investment between small raise and all-in

2 BB small raise versus 15 BB all-in; different risk and subsequent space.

A small raise may preserve fold or turn action space, while all-in concentrates the decision pre-flop. Which hands suit which approach depends on position and opponent reaction, not a fixed threshold.
