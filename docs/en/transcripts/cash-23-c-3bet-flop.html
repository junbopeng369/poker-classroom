Planning the Flop in a 3bet Pot

With the same pre-flop bet size, compare favorable and unfavorable hand types

Takeaway: Low SPR does not mean betting into any pair; even in strong hand types, consider the bet range.

  • Offline 1/3 · 8–9 player table
  • Example hand explicitly notes effective chips
  • Range example is not a general GTO range

1. First reconstruct the pot and remaining chips

BTN opens 10, SB raises to 40, BTN calls, BB folds; pot is 83

Both players start with 300 and have 260 left on the flop, for an SPR of about 3.13. The BB's 3 is dead money; the SB's original blind is already included in the total of 40.

5. JJ has an A-high hand, different situation

Same preflop action, SB holds J♠J♦, flop A♣ 7♦ 2♠.

You lead slightly against small pairs and air, trailing the sharp cards. Checking can serve as a protective starting point for medium pot value, but you cannot automatically bet the pot just because it's a re (re-raise).
