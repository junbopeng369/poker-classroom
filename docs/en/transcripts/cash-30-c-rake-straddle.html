Rake, Straddles and the Real Price

Bring table rules into the calculation to avoid misjudging depth and pot odds.

Takeaway: Range tables depend on actual structure; rake and live blinds alter edge decisions.

  • Offline 1/3 · 8–9 player table
  • Example hand explicitly notes effective chips
  • Range example is not a general GTO range

1. Rake can change edge calls

The pot originally has 30, opponent shoves 15, you need to call 15.

In this example, the total pot is reduced by 5%, capped at 6, with no other fees. After calling, the total pot is 60, rake is 3, and the net win is 57; the threshold becomes 15 divided by 57.
