Current call cost and effective full-raise amount may be less than the opponent's apparent raise amount.

Takeaway: Ante goes into pot but doesn't cover blind; excess chips beyond matchable amount are returned.

7. Blind positions still need to consider action space

When the big blind is short, the small blind's range against a raise or a call will change.

In a chip model without bonus pressure, a good price supports a wider continuation range; but don't forget the opponent's actual raise range and whether you're covered.

8. When entering a tournament, clearly write down three numbers

Starting chips, amount already paid in blinds and ante, and amount still available to match.

This way, you won't mistakenly include the entire all-in amount in the pool you can win. When funds are insufficient to cover an ante, the priority order should not be guessed.
