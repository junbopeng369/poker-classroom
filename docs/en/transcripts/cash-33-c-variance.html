7. Budget cannot fix a negative-expectation strategy

Independent budget, session cap, and downgrade conditions should be set before the hand begins.

No fixed buy-in amount works for everyone to avoid bankruptcy. Personal finances, risk tolerance, and long-term edge vary; implement rest or exit plans when chasing losses or losing judgment occurs.
