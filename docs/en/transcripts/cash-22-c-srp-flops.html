Single-Raised Pots: Common Flop Textures

Categorize the board and connect it to the current hand range, then decide on small or large bet or check.

Takeaway: Board categorization helps generate candidate actions, but does not replace position, range, or opponent response.

BTN opening, BB calling; A♣7♦2♠, you hold A♠Q♠

2. Connected two suits, pair, or flush

9♥8♥7♣ with K♣K♦4♠ and Q♥8♥3♥ have different ranges.

Connected two-suit boards offer many continuation hands. Pair boards reduce available same-rank combinations, but do not imply zero risk. Flush boards should be evaluated separately based on whether a flush is already formed, high card, or no flush.

5. Consider raising when out of position

CO opens the pot and is called by BTN, acting first on each street.

You have less control over how opponents use information, so medium-strength and some strong hands can be included in check-raise ranges. Strong hands checking can help prevent check-raises from appearing weak.
