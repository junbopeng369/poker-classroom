Why Fold When You Have Position?

The same AJo in a different action situation may lead to a different result.

Takeaway: Position is an advantage; whether to continue depends on range, price, and number of players.

Answer and explanations

Suggested answer: Raise to 10
Yes, about 3.3 BB can serve as a starting point for this situation. Specific sizing can be adjusted based on blind reactions.

Alternative: Just call 3
AJo has value in a raise from this position. Start with a basic raise or fold strategy to avoid habitual slow calls.

Alternative: Fold immediately
When everyone else folds, a raise to AJo is often worth it from BTN. Don't transfer the fold against strong ranges to this situation.

Assume the big blind calls and the small blind folds. On the flop, the big blind acts first, then you act.

You raise to 10, the big blind calls to 10, and the small blind folds. On the flop, the big blind acts first on each street, and you see his action before deciding. This is positional advantage.

8. Conditions have changed; reevaluate.

Position × Range × Price × Number of players jointly determine continuation.
