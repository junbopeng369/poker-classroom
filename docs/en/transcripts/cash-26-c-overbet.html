Overbets, Polarization and Three-Street Sizing

Use numbers to understand the value and bluff structure required for a large bet.

Takeaway: The bet size, range structure, and future stack depth must be consistent; MDF is not a required call proportion.

  • Offline 1/3 · 8–9 player table
  • Example hand explicitly notes effective chips
  • Range example is not a general GTO range

1. Why polarized ranges can make large bets

Strong value hands and low bluff value bluffs against medium-range bluff catchers.

If the opponent's range contains fewer top hands and you have a strong value portion, you can study large or overpot bets. Do not simply increase medium hands to 'make him suffer'.

5. MDF differs from the call threshold

Pot 100, bet 75: minimum defense frequency model approximately 57.1%, single hand call threshold is 30%.

The first comes from a simplified model preventing automatic profit from zero-win bluffs, where defense may include raises; the second compares a hand's win rate. Against players who under-bluff, one can reasonably fold more hands.
