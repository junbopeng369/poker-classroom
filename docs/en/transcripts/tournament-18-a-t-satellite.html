5. Cannot generalize to all tournaments

If the prize is not equivalent to a ticket, or if short stack, blinds, or action order changes, the conclusion changes.

It is especially important to retain clear assumptions. Do not just remember 'satellite tournaments should fold strong hands'—instead, check the current advancement probability and how much equity is gained by taking further risks.
