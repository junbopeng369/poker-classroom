Small Blind and Big Blind: Price and Position

Practice small blind opening, big blind defending, and blind vs blind matchups separately.

Takeaway: Big blind has discount, small blind often has no position; neither can use discount nor hand names to replace range evaluation.

  • Offline 1/3 · 8–9 player table
  • Example hand explicitly notes effective chips
  • Range example is not a general GTO range

4. Big blind vs. button 3 BB raise to the pot

Small blind folds; big blind must add 2 BB to call; the total pot before rake is 6.5 BB.

The nominal threshold is 2 divided by 6.5, about 30.8%. But there is more betting to come and you are out of position. Rake and equity realization affect which hands can profitably call.

7. Facing the early position, you must also tighten

Under the same bet size, the tight range from early position differs from the wide range at the button.

You must be sensitive to price and also to your opponent's opening range. Especially weak suited high cards are easily dominated; high card suited hands with nut potential should be evaluated based on the cost to call.
