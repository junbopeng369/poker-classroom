Regular, Satellite and Bounty Tournaments

First confirm the reward distribution method, then select a strategy.

Takeaway: Different tournament formats alter the reward function and cannot share an unadjusted ICM or continuation bet strategy.

  • Chips and ante as per problem statement

1. Standard tournaments: prize distribution based on finish position

Different finish positions typically have different rewards; chip count affects future position probabilities.

The earlier prize model of 50, 30 and 20 uses this simplified structure. Real tournaments may also have re-entry periods, different speeds and special rules; check each before entering.

2. Satellite tournaments: the goal may be identical tickets

If three players remain for two tickets of equal value, the first and second-place rewards are the same.

Adding more chips may not significantly increase the chance of advancing, but the risk of elimination may be high. Focus on the probability of securing a ticket, not on copying standard tournament first-place strategies.

3. Cash game: elimination rewards add an additional gain
