Complete Hand: The Limits of Deep-Stack QQ

When in position and calling, update continuously on each street.

Takeaway: One call does not commit to future actions; on the river, compare current range to price only.

  • Offline 1/3 · 8–9 player table
  • Example hand explicitly notes effective chips
  • Range example is not a general GTO range
