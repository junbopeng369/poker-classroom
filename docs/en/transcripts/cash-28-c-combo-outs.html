Combo Draws, Dirty Outs and Redraws

Before counting draws, confirm no duplicates and that the draw is actually useful upon completion.

Takeaway: Probability of hitting vs. final equity differs; the error of the 2-4 rule increases significantly with many additional cards.

4. Hitting a flush may still result in a loss

Turn A♥K♥2♣3♦; you have 8♥7♥, and the opponent has Q♥J♥.

Both players hold two hearts, and the opponent's two cards are higher. On the river, both players can make a flush, but the opponent's flush is larger. Do not consider these hearts as clean winning cards for you.

7. There's also catching up and redraw potential

A set against a flush draw on the flop, even if the turn is caught, may still become a full house on the river.

Therefore, treating the number of outs on a single street as the final equity may be incorrect. When practicing, note how many cards, opponent range, dirty outs, and future developments.
