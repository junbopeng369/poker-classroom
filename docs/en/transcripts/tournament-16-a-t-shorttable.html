7. Adjust against an aggressive big blind

If the BB frequently attacks limp entries, it will be harder to realize equity with weak non-flush draws.

Can reduce some marginal bluff catches and allow stronger hands to enter the continuation range after a 3-bet; cannot simply add bet all strong hands and limp all weak hands to expose the pattern.

8. Boundary for a single-table no-ante table

A Nash equilibrium table for two players with no ante and pure shove-fold does not equal a complete strategy for more than two players.

With ante, more players behind, a limp, or a small raise, the game has changed. The public head-to-head table can serve as a reference model, but must first verify the allowed actions and inputs.
