Antes, Stealing Blinds and the Price of Defending

Use the initial pot to understand why tournaments require active pot pursuit.

Takeaway: The big blind ante increases the pot, keeping BB unchanged in conversion; both opening and defense must be re-evaluated based on new pot sizes.

  • Live tournament
  • Chips and ante as per problem statement
  • Strategy must align with reward structure

1. Action before 5, 000

Blind 1,000/2,000, big blind pays an additional 2,000 ante.

Under all conditions of mandatory full payment, the initial pot is 1,000+2,000+2,000=5,000. Chips are still calculated based on the big blind 2,000, not 4,000.

Full spoken explanation

Under all conditions of mandatory full payment, the initial pot is one thousand plus two thousand plus two thousand equals five thousand. Chips are still calculated based on the big blind two thousand, not four thousand.

2. Later position opening to capture more dead money

BTN raises to 4,000; if all opponents fold, the additional gain is 5,000.

This explains how the antes change the incentive to fight for the pot. The specific opening range still depends on effective stack, blind reactions, position, and bonus pressure, and does not mean that a blind should be infinitely widened just because there is an ante.

3. The big blind call price is also changed

The big blind has posted 2,000 blinds and is facing a raise to 4,000. Only an additional 2,000 is needed to call.

If the small blind folds, after the big blind calls, the pot becomes 5,000 + 4,000 + 2,000 = 11,000. This price is attractive, but the big blind is out of position post-flop and cannot automatically call all hands. The ante does not count as a deduction against the current call blind.
