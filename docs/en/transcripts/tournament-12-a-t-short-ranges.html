8. A separate table for calling an opponent's all-in

When tight in early position, you don't have the benefit of making an opponent fold.

The training table's call-to-shove core assumes a tight, defined range. First calculate the call price, then consider the actions of players behind and the pot equity; do not assume that any hand can call an opponent's all-in just because it can shove.
