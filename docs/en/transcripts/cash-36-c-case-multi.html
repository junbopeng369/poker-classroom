Complete Hand: Multiway Top Pair Facing Short-Stack Shoves

When the pot is large, still compare actual equity to price.

Takeaway: First confirm if there are any further bets, then use direct win probability thresholds.

  • Offline 1/3 · 8–9 player table
  • Example hand explicitly notes effective chips
  • Range example is not a general GTO range

1. Three players in the pot, two opponents with shorter stacks

1/3, you start with 300, the other two each with 72. You open 12, both follow, blind folds.

You hold ace-king and the flop pot is 40. Each opponent has 60 left, and you cover them. You are not playing a full 100 BB confrontation against each opponent.
