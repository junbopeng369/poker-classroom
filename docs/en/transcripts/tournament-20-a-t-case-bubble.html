Complete Bubble Hand: Calling a Shorter Stack’s Shove

Combine blind antes, existing commitment, and ICM into one example.

Takeaway: ICM should compare the actual post-action stack results, not just apply a percentage to the pot.

  • Live tournament
  • Chips and ante as per problem statement
  • Strategy must align with reward structure

1. Four-player bubble specific opening hands

Starting stacks: 50k, 25k, 15k, 10k; prizes: 50, 30, 20, 0.

The blinds are 500/1,000, with a 1,000 BB ante. The early-position player with 50,000 folds. You are on the BTN with 25,000 and open AJo to 2,000. The SB with 15,000 folds. The BB started with 10,000 and shoves, making a total live wager of 9,000 this betting round.
