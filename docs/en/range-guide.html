Preflop range baselines

These are original, conditional teaching baselines, not solver solutions or a claim of maximum expected value. Match the number of players, effective stack, open size, rake and opponent assumptions before using a chart. Yellow cells require the stated evidence; they are not fixed mixed-strategy frequencies. Table coverage is not hand equity.

s = suited; o = offsuit. Pocket pairs have six combinations, suited hands four and offsuit hands twelve before known-card removal. Heads-up small-blind calling may mean completing the blind; elsewhere a call responds to a raise.

UTG first raise · 100 BB

No-limit Hold’em; 9-handed; 100 BB effective; no ante or straddle; unopened pot. Open to about 3 BB, or about 3.5–4 BB from SB. Illustrative rake: 5%, capped at 2 BB. A conservative teaching baseline, not GTO.

ActionHands
raise55+,A9s+,KTs+,QTs+,JTs,T9s,98s,AJo+,KQo
  • Green indicates a raise in the simplified baseline; gray defaults to fold.
  • 200 BB does not automatically expand this opening raise table; depth adjustments should refer to the dedicated table for raises against raises.
  • On an 8-player table, count positions forward from the button: UTG references this table at UTG+1, UTG+1 references at UTG+2, all others remain unchanged.
  • Actual rake, raise size, or different opponents require re-evaluation of marginal hands.

BTN faces a 3bet from the same position · 200 BB

BTN opens to 10 and SB 3bets to 40, with 600 effective. Assume SB has a reasonable non-nut 3bet range containing enough AQ and suited broadways. After a call, the pot is 83 and each player has 560 left, for an SPR of about 6.75.
